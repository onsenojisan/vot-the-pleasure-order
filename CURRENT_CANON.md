## Current Verdict

VOT / The Pleasure Order is a testable research program, not a validated distinct theory. Its measurable order operator was absorbed by a prediction-error comparator across four open EMA datasets. The remaining collapse-gate wager is un-run. The core claim is therefore undecided and presently trends toward synthesis/redescription rather than distinctiveness.

## Current Terms

- `p_loc`: felt-consonance appearance channel.
- `C1`: internal configural consonance.
- `r_C+` / `r_C-`: structural formation/collapse rates; not definitions of felt pleasure/suffering.
- `Omega`: domain-, horizon-, and perturbation-indexed trajectory-revision capacity; not value itself.
- `OmegaHat` / `Y_Omega`: an estimator or endpoint, not direct access to Omega.

See the [Terminology Contract](TERMINOLOGY_CONTRACT.md). Earlier `pleasure = dC/dt` wording is historical and not current empirical terminology.

## Current Test Discipline

- D4 without P3 and D5 cannot upgrade the core claim.
- A fold-like result supports structure only; it does not by itself support Ω, value, or VOT distinctiveness.
- A properly powered non-fold result must be allowed to count against the gate.
- A test that cannot distinguish `UNDERPOWERED` from `NO_STRUCTURE` is inconclusive.
- VOT must beat explicit, estimable strong FEP/active-inference rivals, not only a generic prediction-error proxy.
