# Terminology Contract — Philosophy, Structure And Measurement

Status: **current public terminology rule**
Date: 2026-08-15
Claim level: editorial / construct discipline; no empirical upgrade

## Purpose

The corpus previously used `pleasure`, `suffering`, `dC/dt`, felt consonance and
structural consonance in overlapping ways. This contract gives each term one job
on the public empirical surface. Historical and frozen records retain their
wording, but current documents are interpreted through this file.

## Canonical Terms

| Term | Current meaning | Explicitly not |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary felt pleasure / suffering | first-person appearance channels, measured through report or another declared appearance measure | structural `C`, `dC/dtau`, Omega or value |
| `p_loc` | felt/reported local consonance; an appearance-side proxy | whole-system structural consonance |
| `C1` | internal configural consonance among a frozen set of channels | the harmful-order gate or Omega |
| `r_C+`, `r_C-` | technical rates of structural consonance formation and collapse | ordinary felt pleasure and suffering |
| `C2` | anti-rigidity / harmful-coherence property, declared Omega-aligned by construction | an independent measure of Omega |
| `Omega` | descriptive trajectory-revision / self-recovery capacity in a declared domain, horizon and perturbation set | value, goodness, welfare, fitness or a ranking of lives |
| `Y_Omega` / `OmegaHat` | analyst's estimate from a predeclared independent external functional or viability endpoint | Omega itself, a self-report relabel, or an individual counterfactual |
| value | a separate philosophical / appearance concept; not scored by Omega | `Omega`, viability ordering or positive directionality |
| `d/dtau` | derivative with respect to an empirical time parameter whose units are declared in the study | the philosophy core's account of time in F11 |

## Rules

1. Current empirical documents do not write `pleasure = dC/dt` or `suffering =
   -dC/dt`, even as a technical identity. They may test an association between
   felt channels and `r_C+` / `r_C-`.
2. `dC/dt` in a frozen or historical document is read contextually. New
   specifications use `DeltaC1`, `r_C+`, `r_C-` or another construct-specific
   symbol rather than asking one symbol to carry structural and appearance jobs.
3. C2 and the harmful-order gate cannot serve as evidence that is independent of
   Omega. They are explanatory predictors and must beat their components alone
   against a distal external endpoint.
4. `positive directionality` means only a signed change in a predeclared
   descriptive endpoint. It does not mean good, valuable or morally preferred.
5. Terms such as `harmful`, `ruin`, `Hell`, `healthy` and `disordered` require an
   explicit standpoint: clinical convention, participant preference, external
   functional criterion, or a hypothetical aim such as avoiding self-collapse.
   They are not licensed by Omega alone.
6. Population-level contrasts are reported at population level. No individual
   counterfactual is inferred without an identified individual-level design.

## Cross-Layer Reading

The philosophy core may discuss functional correlates of feeling while remaining
agnostic about qualia. On the empirical surface those correlates are named
`r_C+` / `r_C-`; the words pleasure and suffering remain appearance terms. This
preserves the philosophical observation limit without turning an unverified
correlate into the measured phenomenon.

## Historical Documents

The following phrases are historical and may appear only inside an explicit
quotation, correction record or superseded document:

- `pleasure = coherence`;
- `pleasure = dC/dt` as an identity;
- `suffering = -dC/dt` as an identity;
- `value = Omega`;
- value is tied to preservation or recovery of directionality;
- Omega ranks lives, persons or groups.
